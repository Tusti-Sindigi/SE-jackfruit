## Objective

Define the requirements for the Donation and Appointment Management module.

## Scope

The requirements should cover:

- Booking a donation appointment
- Viewing appointments
- Rescheduling appointments
- Cancelling appointments
- Staff management of appointments
- Recording completed blood donations
- Linking donations to donors
- Recording donation details
- Updating inventory after a completed donation

## Expected Output

- Clearly defined functional requirements
- Relevant business rules
- Assumptions and TBD items
- Unique requirement IDs
- Requirements that are clear and testable

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] Appointment requirements are documented
- [ ] Donation requirements are documented
- [ ] Relevant business rules are identified
- [ ] TBD items are identified
- [ ] Requirement IDs are assigned
- [ ] Requirements are ready to be included in the SRS
